# 만능 스토리 전략 분석

> **목적**: 소리담 승인 기출을 바탕으로, 최소 개수의 가상(외우는) 스토리로 최대 질문을 커버하는 OPIc 만능 답변 전략을 도출.
> **대상 학습자**: 전진성 (IM2~IH 경계 → 목표 AL · 강점=순발력·대화지속 · 약점=주제유지·시제·기본문법)
> **제품 연결**: `/coaching` "만능 스토리" 탭 (`components/coaching/story-browser.tsx`), 코칭 "시제 만능 아크", 스크립트 "만능 패턴/도입(opening)"

|---|---|---|---|

## 1. 결론

**✅ 강력하게 가능. 단 정확히는 — 만능 = "IH 바닥 보장 + 시제·구조 봉합 장치". AL '돌파'는 만능 + 변주 + 즉흥이 같이 가야 한다.**

## 2. 핵심 통찰 — 만능의 2대 축

past_* 문항 전체가 단 2개의 드라마로 양분된다.

```
        모든 past_* (특별/최근/어릴적) + 롤플레이 past_special + rp_12
                              │
          ┌───────────────────┴───────────────────┐
          ▼                                        ▼
   축①  긍정 에피소드                       축②  문제해결 에피소드
   "인생 최고의 순간"                        "고장 → 대응 → 해결"
   여행·콘서트·자연체험                       MP3·가전·은행·집·교통
   과거시제 일관 (락인)                       과거시제 + rp_12는 could 전환
```

### 왜 이 학습자에게 특히 맞나

| 약점 | 만능 2대 축의 처방 |
|---|---|
| 🔴 시제 일관성 | 드라마 통째 암기 → 과거시제가 물리적으로 고정 |
| 🔴 주제 유지(횡설수설) | 7~8비트 기→승→전→결 순서가 머릿속 체크리스트 → 샛길 차단 |
| 🟢 순발력(강점) | 본문은 외운 대로, 도입 한 문장만 즉석 변형 → 강점은 도입에만, 약점은 암기로 봉합 |

## 4. 실제 가상 스토리 아크

> 영어는 IH~AL 구어체. **🛡️ 방어 anchor** = "tell me everything" 꼬리질문 대비 디테일(같이 외워야 안 무너짐). 실제 콘텐츠는 `lib/data/universal-stories.json` 참조.

### 0. 자기소개 — 1번 문항 100% 고정
```
1. Hi, my name is Jinseong, and I'm in my early thirties living in Korea.
2. I work as an office worker, so my weekdays are pretty busy.
3. But when I get free time, I'm really into music — I listen to it every single day.
4. I also love traveling, especially short trips around Korea to clear my head.
5. People say I'm outgoing and talkative, so I make friends pretty easily.
6. These days I'm working hard on my English speaking — that's why I'm here today.
```
3번(음악)·4번(여행) 떡밥 → 아크 C·A로 자연 연결. "talkative"는 주제유지 약점을 캐릭터로 덮는 장치.

### 🅰 "제주 인생여행" — 긍정 에피소드
```
1. A couple of years ago, I went to Jeju Island with three close friends.
2. The first thing we did was climb Hallasan — honestly my legs were killing me.
3. But when we reached the top, the view was absolutely breathtaking.
4. After that, we drove along the coast and spent the afternoon at a quiet beach.
5. At night, we stayed at a cozy guesthouse, and the owner cooked us fresh seafood.
6. We stayed up almost all night, talking and laughing.
7. What made it so special was that it was my first real trip without my parents.
```
🛡️ anchor: 동행("Minjun, my friend since high school + two from college") / 계절("early summer, warm but breezy") / 사고("on the way down it rained and we got soaked — couldn't stop laughing")

| 질문 | 도입만 교체 |
|---|---|
| 국내여행 unforgettable | "The most unforgettable trip I've taken was to Jeju…" |
| 지형 natural place | "One of the most memorable natural places was Hallasan…" |
| 해외여행 first trip abroad | "My first trip abroad was Osaka…" (비트2~6 일본식 명사 스왑) |
| 호텔 last stay | "The last time I stayed somewhere was this guesthouse…" |

🟢 안전: 국내·해외여행·지형·집에서휴가·호텔·휴일·자유시간 / 🔴 금지: 사물·상황 명시 past_special(가구·은행 문제) → 아크 B로

### 🅱 "그날 다 고장났다" — 문제해결 ⭐최대 레버
```
1. A while ago, I had a really stressful experience with [my laptop / MP3 / bank card].
2. It happened on a morning when I had something important to do — worst timing ever.
3. At first I thought it was a small glitch, so I tried turning it off and on — nothing.
4. I started to panic because I needed it right then.
5. So I [called the service center / went straight to the store] and explained everything.
6. After some back and forth, they finally figured out the problem and fixed it.
7. In the end it worked out, but it was one of the most frustrating mornings I've had.
```
🛡️ anchor: 뭐가/왜("the screen went black — the battery connector was loose") / 감정("I was sweating, I had a presentation in two hours") / 해결("replaced the part on the spot, only took 20 minutes")

| 질문 | 비트1·5 스왑 |
|---|---|
| MP3 고장 | "…my MP3, it stopped right before a long trip" |
| 가전 broken | "…my washing machine, it broke with clothes still wet" |
| 은행 카드/ATM | "…my bank card, the ATM swallowed it" → 5: "went to the bank counter" |
| 집 something broke | "…the water heater stopped in mid-winter" → 5: "called a repair person" |
| 교통 breakdown | "…the subway broke down, I was stuck underground" |

🔴 반드시 질문 토픽에 맞춰 명사 스왑. "가구" 물었는데 "MP3"로 답하면 토픽 미스매치 감점.

### 🅲 "그 콘서트 밤" — 음악/공연 (최고빈도)
```
1. About a year ago, I went to my first-ever live concert with my best friend.
2. We bought the tickets months in advance, counting down the days.
3. When the lights went down and the music started, the whole crowd exploded — I got chills.
4. We sang along to every song at the top of our lungs.
5. My favorite moment was when they played the song that got me into music.
6. After the show we grabbed food and talked about it for hours.
```
🛡️ anchor: 밴드명("an indie rock band called…")·곡명·장소("Olympic Hall in Seoul") 미리 확정.

### 🅳 "우리 집 & 동네" — 묘사 허브 (⚠️ 틀1 + 토픽카드)
```
1. I live in a cozy apartment in [동네], not huge but really comfortable.
2. It has two bedrooms, a living room, a kitchen, and a bathroom.
3. My favorite spot is the living room — it gets a lot of sunlight and I relax there.
4. There's a big comfy sofa, a TV, and a little shelf full of my stuff.
5. The neighborhood is convenient — there's a [bank / restaurant] right around the corner.
```
장소 스왑(비트4~5) + 토픽당 명사·형용사 3~5개 카드. ⚠️ 빈 틀만으론 AL 내용밀도 미달.

### 🅴 롤플레이 쌍
**E1 · rp_11 (전화 질문) — 🟡 medium** (순발력으로 메우는 구간)
```
1. Hi! I heard about [the box office / your gym], and I wanted to ask a few questions.
2. First, could you tell me how much it costs, and are there any discounts?
3. Second, I was wondering about [availability / what's included] for next weekend?
4. Also, is there anything I need to prepare beforehand?
5. That's everything — thanks so much!
```
의문문 5종(가격/위치/시간/조건/예약) 외우고 객체에 맞는 3~4개 즉석 선택.

**E2 · rp_12 (사과+대안) — ✅ 점수 잘 나오는 만능틀**
```
1. Hey [name], it's me — I'm so sorry, but I have a bit of a problem.
2. (과거) The thing is, [I broke your MP3 / I woke up sick] this morning.
3. I feel really bad, and I know this might be inconvenient.
4. (could) So here's what I'm thinking — first, I could [buy you a new one / reschedule].
5. (could) Or, we could [get it repaired / meet some other time].
6. Let me know which works better — I'm totally flexible.
```
🔑 시제 락인: 비트2 = 과거형(broke/woke up), 비트4~5 = could + 동사원형.

### 🅵 "예전엔 ~ 지금은" — 변화 서사
```
1. [Music / Banking] has changed so much compared to when I was younger.
2. (used to) Back then, we used to [buy CDs / go to the bank in person].
3. I remember [carrying a CD player / waiting in long lines], unbelievable now.
4. (these days) But these days, everything is [on our phones / done with an app].
5. Now I can just [stream any song / transfer money in seconds] from home.
```
🔑 비트2(과거 used to) ↔ 비트4(현재 these days) 대조 = 시제 전환 훈련 그 자체.

## 5. 배경설문 선택 전략

같은 만능 스토리로 묶이게 클러스터로 몰아 선택.

| 클러스터 | 함께 고를 토픽 | 묶이는 아크 |
|---|---|---|
| 🎵 엔터 | 음악 + 영화 + TV (+ MP3) | C + D + F |
| ✈️ 여행 | 국내여행 + 해외여행 + 집에서휴가 (+지형·호텔) | A + D |
| 🏠 주거 | 집 + 가전 + 가구 (+부동산) | B + D + E2 |

⚠️ 현실 제약: ① 카테고리별 최소 선택 개수 제약으로 "만능 토픽만 100%"는 불가 ② 공통형 ~40%(2콤보)는 통제 불가 — 지형·재활용·미용실 등 만능 적합도 낮은 게 빈출 → 공통형은 빈도순 학습(`빈도분석`: 공통형 상위10 = 59.7% 커버).

## 6. 만능이 안 통하는 구간 (정직하게)

- **🔴 adv_15 (사회이슈) — 유일한 진짜 저항.** 아카이브 38 인스턴스, 토픽마다 다른 시사 콘텐츠 필수. 틀(`"Recently there's been talk about X → people worry Y → I think Z"`)은 빌리되 빈 틀은 거의 0점. 선택 묶음 맞춤 시사카드 2~3장 집중(음악=무선이어폰, 인터넷=개인정보, 전화기=대면소통). AL 목표면 생략 불가.
- **🟡 rp_11 / adv_14 — medium.** 틀은 있으나 즉석 조합·추상화 필요. 강점(순발력)으로 커버.
- **🚨 "외운 티" / 꼬리질문 리스크.** OPIc는 직전 답변 기반 follow-up. 외운 비트만 흘리다 디테일 추궁당하면 무너짐 → 각 아크의 🛡️ anchor를 같이 외워 즉흥 확장 가능하게.
- **🚨 AL 'rehearsed pattern' 천장.** 채점자가 암기 템플릿을 감지하면 AL 상한. 같은 여행 드라마가 4토픽 반복 시 "레퍼토리 부족"으로 읽힘 → 핵심 아크는 2~3개 변주 버전, 톤은 낭독 말고 필러("you know", "honestly") 섞기.

> **재포지셔닝**: 만능 = "IH 바닥 보장 도구". AL 돌파는 만능 + 변주 + 즉흥 + 내용밀도가 같이. **커버리지(뭐가 나오나) ≠ 등급(수행 품질).**

## 7. 외울 순서 (ROI 순)

```
1주차 ▶ ① 자기소개 (쉽고 100% 적중, 워밍업)
        ② 🅱 문제해결 드라마  ★최대 레버 — past_*+rp_12 동시, 시제 락인
2주차 ▶ ③ 🅰 긍정 드라마 (고빈도 여행·체험)
        ④ 🅲 콘서트 (전체 최고빈도, 제일 두껍게)
```
